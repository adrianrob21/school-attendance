# Seasonal meadow background prompts

Generation method: built-in `image_gen` tool, one separate edit per season.
Reference for every season: [original meadow background](../public/assets/backgrounds/group-selection-meadow.png).
Purpose: four separate opaque backgrounds with the same composition and a quiet center for the app's content.

## Spring

Asset: [group-selection-spring.png](../public/assets/backgrounds/group-selection-spring.png).

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit / seasonal-background-variant
Asset type: standalone background illustration for a child-friendly kindergarten attendance React app.
Reference/edit target: the approved original meadow background image.
Create ONE full-canvas seasonal version of this exact background, for SPRING only. Do not create a collage, split scene, panels, labels, or four-season montage.

Preserve the same 1536 × 1024 landscape framing, same storybook watercolor and gouache style, gentle paper texture, cheerful rounded forms, and approximately the same spatial arrangement. The top-left overhanging branch, smiling sun in the upper-right, soft clouds around the upper outer edges, trees at the outer left and right edges, low U-shaped meadow border along the bottom, wooden fence at bottom-left, and stones at bottom-right must stay in their established locations. This must visibly be the same place in another season.

Preserve the very large quiet light cream open center and upper-middle for app content. Do not put any tree, horizon, characters, buildings, text, symbols or large decorations into the empty center. Keep all seasonal decorative detail confined to the outer edges and low bottom border. Keep the bottom border's U-shaped inner contour as close to the original as possible.

SPRING: a fresh early-spring garden meadow. Transform the upper-left branch into delicate young light-green leaves, small pink-and-white blossoms and buds. Trees at the outer edges have fresh green growth and a few pale blossoms. Change the low meadow edge to tender vivid spring-green grass with tasteful small clusters of pink tulips and pale yellow daffodils and a few daisies, all within the existing plant border. A few tiny petals may drift near the far sides only. Soft bright morning light, pastel blue clouds, a gentle warm yellow smiling sun, cream center. Keep the fence and rocks visible. Make the spring blossoms and tulips clearly recognizable without adding visual clutter.

Constraints: cheerful and reassuring for small children, never gloomy. Background illustration only: no UI, text, lettering, labels, group cards, buttons, arrows, logos, pagination, watermark, animal/insect mascots, people, or new buildings. No transparent areas. Preserve the original smiling sun's friendly identity; adapt only its seasonal warmth. Do not add clothing or props to the sun. Output a single opaque full-size PNG at 1536 × 1024.
```
